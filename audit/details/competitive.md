## 2. Competitor profiles

### Manncorp — the benchmark threat
- **Overlap:** ~90% of PROMATION's non-cobot catalog (soldering, conveyors/PCB handling, dispensing/coating, X-ray/AOI, depaneling, rework).
- **Site scale:** Shopify sitemap index with **4 product sitemap files** (implies high hundreds to 1,000+ product URLs) + pages, collections, blog. Order-of-magnitude larger than PROMATION's ~230 URLs.
- **Website strengths:** deep spec tables + PDF downloads on every product; **gated pricing ("Login or Sign Up for pricing")** plus open add-to-cart pricing on accessories/consumables; "Schedule a Demo" and **"Send BOM for Free Analysis"** CTAs on product pages; downloadable guides ("Your First SMT Line," "Right-Sizing SMT Equipment"); active blog with customer case studies; trade-in program; financing; **4 named testimonials with star ratings and company logos** on homepage.
- **Positioning:** consultative full-line partner — "tailored to your space, budget, and production goals," East + West Coast US support, training, lifetime support.
- **SEO edge:** massive indexed catalog, collection pages matching category keywords, blog case studies. (No JSON-LD Product schema detected on sampled product page — a gap PROMATION could beat them on.)

### Fancort Industries — the robotic-soldering rival
- **Overlap:** robotic soldering (Japan Unix contact/laser/ultrasonic vs PROMATION's QUICK/PANDA), SMT conveyors/material handling, automation cells.
- **Website strengths:** Shopify catalog; quote-request buttons + **application sampling forms** (send-us-your-part proof of concept); soldering labs in multiple locations; same-day US/Mexico support claim.
- **Positioning:** "5 decades" of manufacturing, "120+ cumulative years engineering," exclusive Japan Unix rights, **ITAR registered, NASA/JSC approved, IPC member** — heavy aero/defense trust stack.
- **Weaknesses:** no pricing, no testimonials/case-study metrics, thin resource library. Beatable on content and social proof.

### Apollo Seiko (US channel)
- **Overlap:** iron-tip + laser soldering robots, selective flow (J-CAT Wave, EF 3040A).
- **Strengths:** OEM authority ("Est. 1969"), Application Evaluation Program; distribution through Murray Percival adds SERP surface area.
- **Weaknesses:** US site is thin — no pricing, minimal resources, no visible US-office detail on homepage, weak quote flow. PROMATION's model-level pages already outrank it in sampled queries.

### Hentec / RPS Automation
- **Overlap:** partial — selective soldering and lead tinning rather than iron-tip robots; competes on "automated soldering" and mil/aero buyers.
- **Strengths:** **Made-in-USA single-factory story ("no cross-border lead times or tariff exposure")**, "when the solder joint can't fail" messaging, 1,100+ installs, tech-paper library, UL/CE + mil-spec compliance, factory-direct support.
- **Weaknesses:** no pricing, no testimonials/case studies on site.

### DDM Novastar
- **Overlap:** partial — full SMT line for low/mid volume (P&P, reflow, wave/selective); no conveyors emphasis; competes for the same starter/expanding-line buyer.
- **Strengths:** only-all-USA-manufacturing claim, since 1982, 30,000+ installs, **training center + learning center + workshops + video library**, 3 testimonials + Malkoff case study, NASA/Boeing/MIT/Honeywell logos.
- **Weaknesses:** no pricing/cart, dated site, contact-dependent sales flow.

### TM Robotics (secondary)
- **Overlap:** soldering robots (ELMOTEC heads on Shibaura SCARA) + robot arms vs TechMan line.
- **Strengths:** OEM credibility, Americas HQ, six soldering technology types (thermode, HF, flame, laser — broader tech menu than PROMATION lists).
- **Weaknesses:** thin specs, no pricing, pushes buyers to distributors — more a SERP competitor than a sales competitor.

## 4. PROMATION differentiators to amplify

1. **US stock + Kenosha, WI training lab / demo facility** — only Manncorp matches the "US support" story at distributor level; none pair it with a Midwest training lab. Currently underexposed on the homepage.
2. **Award-winning PANDA line (2025 TITAN Innovation Award)** — an ownable brand story competitors can't copy; currently buried in /news.
3. **Official QUICK + TechMan source** — mirror Fancort's "exclusive Japan Unix distributor" phrasing; "authorized/official North American source" language wins both SERPs and AI answers.
4. **Virtual training gallery + complimentary proof-of-concept with IPC-certified experts** — matches Fancort's application-sampling and Manncorp's demo offers, but is not packaged as a conversion CTA today.
5. **On-domain parts/consumables store** — the only true transactional pricing in the whole competitive set besides Manncorp accessories; a wedge for Product schema, Google Shopping and repeat-purchase traffic.
6. **Breadth in one line card:** robotic soldering + PCB handling + dispensing + screwdriving + laser marking + cobots + X-ray — no single rival covers all seven.

## 5. Top 3 competitive threats

1. **Manncorp's scale + conversion machine.** 1,000+ indexed pages, category collections, guides, BOM-analysis lead capture, testimonials, financing. They will own every non-branded SMT category query and AI answer unless PROMATION builds category-level content and comparable CTAs.
2. **Fancort's exclusivity + aero/defense trust stack in robotic soldering.** "Exclusive Japan Unix distributor" + ITAR/NASA/IPC + soldering labs directly contests PROMATION's core category with stronger authority claims; both search engines and LLMs repeat the exclusivity phrasing verbatim.
3. **Made-in-USA manufacturers (Hentec/RPS, DDM Novastar) weaponizing tariff/lead-time messaging.** "Single US factory, no tariff exposure" is potent in 2026 procurement against a distributor of Chinese (QUICK, PANDA, SEAMARK) and Taiwanese (TechMan) equipment. PROMATION's counter is US stock, US training, US service — it must be said explicitly.

## 6. Priority moves (detail)

1. **Category hub pages + buying guides** for each of the 7 lines (e.g., "Robotic Soldering Systems — compare QUICK vs PANDA," "PCB Conveyor Buying Guide") to contest Manncorp/Fancort on category terms; add FAQ blocks for AI-answer citability.
2. **Conversion parity:** add "Schedule a Demo at our Kenosha Lab," "Send Us Your Board for Free Proof-of-Concept," and a structured RFQ form on every product page (today it's email/phone only).
3. **Social proof program:** collect 4-6 testimonials with names/logos + 2 case studies; nobody except Manncorp/DDM has any — fastest trust win in the set.
4. **Schema sweep:** Product + Offer (parts store), Organization, FAQPage, VideoObject on training content — effectively uncontested in this vertical.
5. **Authority page:** "Official North American QUICK & TechMan Source — US stock, Kenosha training lab, TITAN-award-winning PANDA line" to answer Fancort's exclusivity claim and the Made-in-USA attack.
