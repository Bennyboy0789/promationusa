Overview

The E Series facilitates precise fluid dispensing in a tabletop automated solution. Available in three sizes, from 200 x 200 mm to 500 x 500 mm, the it adapts to a wide range of working envelopes. True 3D motion control makes programming dots, lines, circles, arcs, and compound arcs easier.

Boost Production

Nordson EFD’s E Series automated dispensing system features a simple-to-use Teach Pendant for quick setup and easy programming.

Integrate Easily

The E Series can work as a standalone system or as a key part of an automated solution and is easily integrated into in-line transfer systems, rotary tables, and pallet assembly lines.
